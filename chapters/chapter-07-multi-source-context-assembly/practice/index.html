Chapter 7 Practice Bank ~30 min, hands-on

Practice Bank: Multi-Source Context Assembly

Eight short, independent scenarios, each its own fictional system — none of them Hadleworth Metro Water Authority/ConfluenceLine or Corrinvale Independent Pharmacy Network/ScriptLine again. Each scenario is a few sentences and one judgment or arithmetic question about this chapter's own new skill: deciding which sources belong, ranking their authority, detecting and resolving contradictions, and deduplicating restated content — not deriving a budget or deciding where already-resolved content sits inside the window. The point is speed and accuracy across many different systems.

starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Juniper Ridge Veterinary Partners Judgment

A retrieved vaccination-history document and a live clinic-system tool call, both individually correct, are joined in arrival order with no authority ranking or contradiction check. Does that guarantee the combined result is coherent?

2 Quarrydale Auto Diagnostics Cooperative Production-gear

A retrieved service-history document and a live recall-status tool call disagree about the same claim. Which source wins per the authority ranking?

3 Tamworth Regional Housing Trust Judgment

A team strips exact duplicate sentences across a lease-policy document and the system instructions, with no authority ranking and no reworded-contradiction check. Is that sufficient on its own?

4 Wexford Maritime Charter Group Production-gear

Classify two claim pairs — a charter-fee disagreement and a check-in-location restatement — as genuine contradictions or not.

5 Dovetail Woodcraft Guild Judgment

Deduplication frees a large number of tokens, comfortably inside budget. Does that alone guarantee no contradiction remains?

6 Cinderfield Volunteer Fire Network Production-gear

A claim is asserted by two sources at different token costs. Which source should be kept, and how many tokens does dropping the other save?

7 Barleycroft Grain & Feed Cooperative Production-gear

A naive-vs-recipe regression check on a load-bearing grain-moisture contradiction, including whether the resolved total still fits budget.

8 Yewmarsh Wildlife Sanctuary Judgment

A contradiction is found where both sources share the exact same authority rank, with no clear winner. Safe to ship as-is, or resolve/escalate?
