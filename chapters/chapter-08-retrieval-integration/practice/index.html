Chapter 8 Practice Bank ~30 min, hands-on

Practice Bank: Retrieval Integration

Eight short, independent scenarios, each its own fictional system — none of them Mossgate Regional Law Library Consortium/CiteLine or Cobalt Ridge Claims Adjustment Bureau/DossierLine again. Each scenario is a few sentences and one judgment or arithmetic question about this chapter's own new skill: applying a relevance floor, fitting chunks to budget at a chunk boundary, preserving provenance, stitching adjacent same-document chunks, and handling a low-confidence or empty result — not retrieval architecture itself, and not multi-source contradiction resolution. The point is speed and accuracy across many different systems.

starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Harborlight Maritime Archive Society Judgment

A retrieval pipeline always takes the top 5 ranked chunks regardless of score. Does that guarantee every included chunk is actually relevant?

2 Aspenfield Community College Library Production-gear

Given four scored chunks and a relevance floor, compute which survive.

3 Beacon Crest Genealogy Society Judgment

A pipeline drops chunks below a relevance floor but still truncates survivors by raw character count. Does relevance-floor filtering alone guarantee no mid-sentence cut?

4 Slatebrook Patent Research Group Production-gear

Select surviving chunks in score order until budget is spent, stopping at a chunk boundary rather than truncating mid-chunk.

5 Timberline Structural Engineering Archive Judgment

Two adjacent, individually-accurate chunks from the same report are left un-stitched. Does per-chunk accuracy guarantee a coherent combined passage?

6 Garnet Valley Genetic Testing Registry Production-gear

Decide which of two chunk pairs are consecutive passages of the same document and should be stitched.

7 Poplar Crossing School District Archive Production-gear

A naive-vs-recipe regression check on a load-bearing accommodation clause, including whether the resolved total still fits budget.

8 Otterbend Wildlife Research Station Judgment

Every retrieved chunk scores below the relevance floor. Safe to proceed with the best-available chunk anyway, or surface no relevant result?
