Exercises: Evaluating Context Quality

These exercises use a fresh scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Ternfield Regional Disability Benefits Review Office/ClaimLens hook: Merrivale County Emergency Housing Placement Network, a fictional county agency. Its placement pipeline, PlacementGuard, assembles context for a Housing Placement Agent from four sources — applicant intake, shelter availability, medical needs flags, and prior placement notes — every one of them correctly budgeted, fresh, retrieved, and isolated per Chapters 1-11's own recipes. Your job is this chapter's own new skill: checking the finished bundle itself for completeness, noise, and positional risk, rather than trusting that a correctly-run assembly pipeline guarantees a good result.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total. Six are marked production-gear — a real completeness score, a real noise ratio, a positional audit, a combined quality gate, fix-simulation arithmetic, and a combined classification, not just concept recall.
How to run these Download starter.py, fill in every # TODO, then run python3 starter.py to see an automated score report. Compare against solution.py, which scores a perfect total.
starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Match scenarios to evaluation approaches

Decide which of three PlacementGuard scenarios show no evaluation, proxy-only evaluation, or the full Context Evaluation Recipe.

2 Order the Context Evaluation Recipe

Put the lesson's six recipe steps in the correct sequence, from defining required facts to re-running the evaluation.

3 Production-gear Completeness score

Compute Case MC-5521's own found/required fraction across four required facts.

4 Production-gear Noise ratio

Compute what fraction of the assembled bundle is unrelated material, and whether it clears a 10% threshold.

5 Production-gear Positional audit

Bucket three required facts' own positions in the bundle as front, middle, or back.

6 Production-gear Combined context quality gate

Evaluate three candidate bundles against completeness, noise, and positional rules together.

7 Production-gear Fix-simulation arithmetic

Compute the after-fix token total once noise is removed and a missing fact and a promoted summary are added.

8 Production-gear Combined completeness/quality classification

Classify four final bundles as compliant, completeness_fail, quality_fail, or both_fail.
