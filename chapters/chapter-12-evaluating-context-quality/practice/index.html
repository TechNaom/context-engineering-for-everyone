Practice Bank: Evaluating Context Quality

Eight short, independent scenarios, each its own fictional organization checking an assembled context bundle for completeness, noise, or positional risk — none of them Ternfield Regional Disability Benefits Review Office/ClaimLens or Merrivale County Emergency Housing Placement Network/PlacementGuard again. Each scenario is a few sentences and one judgment or arithmetic question about this chapter's own new skill: fact-level completeness, a real noise ratio, a positional audit, and a combined pre-call quality gate — not ordinary assembly, retrieval, or isolation correctness, which are Chapters 1-11's own already-built territory. The point is speed and accuracy across many different evaluated bundles.

starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Ossbrook Regional Grain Futures Clearinghouse Judgment

Does confirming a required source is present guarantee the specific fact inside it is present too?

2 Colton Regional Home Inspection Licensing Board Production-gear

Compute a real completeness score across five required facts.

3 Bramfield Regional Wildfire Evacuation Coordination Center Judgment

Does fitting a token budget guarantee an acceptable noise ratio?

4 Grendale Regional Court Interpreter Certification Board Production-gear

Compute a real noise ratio and check it against a 10% threshold.

5 Delmoore Regional Pension Fund Audit Office Judgment

Does passing Chapter 11's own two probes guarantee this chapter's own three checks pass too?

6 Sennwick Regional Livestock Export Health Certification Bureau Production-gear

Bucket three required facts' own positions as front, middle, or back.

7 Bexmoor Regional Building Code Variance Board Production-gear

Evaluate three candidate bundles against the combined context quality gate.

8 Warrenfield Regional Small Business Disaster Loan Review Panel Judgment

Does perfect completeness and zero noise guarantee a pass if the one critical fact is bucketed "middle"?
