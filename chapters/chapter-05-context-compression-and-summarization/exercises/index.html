Chapter 5 Exercises ~45 min, hands-on

Exercises: Context Compression and Summarization

These exercises use a second scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Brannigan Home Energy Services/GridLine hook: Kirkholme Public Transit Safety Board, a fictional transit safety oversight agency. Its incident-intake assistant, TransitLine, handles conversations that can run long before a mechanical or safety pattern emerges. Chapter 3's recipe already fired here: the compression trigger and a fixed 480-token summary target are both given. Building the real compression mechanics that decide what survives inside that target is the point.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total. Six are marked production-gear — compression ratio arithmetic, candidate classification, a fidelity check, strategy selection, a naive-vs-fidelity-checked regression gate, and an escalation decision, not just concept recall.
How to run these Download starter.py, fill in every # TODO, then run python3 starter.py to see an automated score report. Compare against solution.py, which scores a perfect total.
starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Match scenarios to compression approaches

Decide which of three TransitLine scenarios need no compression, naive summarization, or the full fidelity-checked pipeline.

2 Order the Compression Fidelity Recipe

Put the lesson's six recipe steps in the correct sequence, from exempting already-pinned content to validating fidelity.

3 Production-gear Compression ratio arithmetic

Compute tokens to cut and retention percentage to hit the 480-token target from a 2,400-token raw segment.

4 Production-gear Load-bearing candidate classification

Decide which incident-report details must be flagged as candidates to preserve before compressing.

5 Production-gear Fidelity check

Find which flagged candidates are missing from a produced summary, and whether the compression passes its own gate.

6 Production-gear Extractive vs. abstractive strategy selection

Classify six content types by whether exact wording or safe paraphrase is the right fit.

7 Production-gear Naive-vs-fidelity-checked regression gate

Confirm a load-bearing technical detail naive compression would drop is correctly preserved by the fidelity-checked pipeline.

8 Production-gear Escalation decision

For three fidelity-check outcomes, decide whether to ship the compressed result or retry/escalate.
