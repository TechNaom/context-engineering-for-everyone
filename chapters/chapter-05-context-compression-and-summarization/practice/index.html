Practice Bank: Context Compression and Summarization

Eight short, independent scenarios, each its own fictional system — none of them Brannigan Home Energy Services/GridLine or Kirkholme Public Transit Safety Board/TransitLine again. Each scenario asks one judgment or arithmetic question about the real mechanics of compressing content without losing what a downstream step depends on, not deriving a compression target itself.

Practice standard Four judgment-call scenarios, four production-gear arithmetic scenarios, across eight fresh fictional organizations.
starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Lynhaven Community Health Partners Judgment

Does compressing a long conversation automatically lose load-bearing facts, or only when there's no fidelity check?

2 Sablewood Legal Trust Production-gear

Compression ratio arithmetic against a fixed token target.

3 Coalridge Municipal Transit Authority Judgment

Is "ask the model to summarize and trust the result" a sufficient compression policy on its own?

4 Pikestone Logistics Group Production-gear

Find missing candidates in a produced summary and whether the fidelity check passes.

5 Rowancraig Insurance Underwriters Judgment

Does an extractive strategy matter more for structured or narrative content?

6 Draymoor Agricultural Cooperative Production-gear

An extractive-vs-abstractive strategy call on a specific technical content type.

7 Osprey Ridge Wealth Management Production-gear

A naive-vs-fidelity-checked regression check on a specific dollar figure.

8 Talmarsh Veterinary Alliance Judgment

If a fidelity check fails once, is it safe to just retry the exact same naive prompt again unchanged?
